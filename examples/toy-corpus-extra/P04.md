# P04. Three-arm trial of dietary counselling intensity in type 2 diabetes

## Abstract
We randomised 150 adults with type 2 diabetes to low-intensity, moderate-intensity or high-intensity dietary counselling. The primary outcome was HbA1c at 24 weeks.

## Methods
### Participants and randomisation
Adults with HbA1c between 7.0% and 9.5% were randomised 1:1:1 by a central web-based system.

### Outcomes
The prespecified primary outcome was HbA1c (%) at 24 weeks. Secondary outcomes were body weight and fasting glucose.

## Results
Six participants were lost to follow-up before 24 weeks and are not included in the analysed population.

Table 1. Participant flow by arm

| Arm | Randomised | Analysed (ITT) |
|---|---|---|
| Low intensity | 50 | 48 |
| Moderate intensity | 50 | 47 |
| High intensity | 50 | 49 |
| Total | 150 | 144 |

Compared with low intensity, HbA1c at 24 weeks was lower by 0.3% in the moderate arm and by 0.6% in the high-intensity arm.
