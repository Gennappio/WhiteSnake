# P03. Mindfulness-based pain management for chronic low back pain: a pragmatic randomised trial

## Abstract
We enrolled 150 adults with chronic low back pain and randomised them to an eight-week mindfulness programme or usual primary care. The primary outcome was pain intensity on a 0 to 10 numeric rating scale at 8 weeks.

## Methods
### Participants
Adults aged 18 to 70 with low back pain for at least three months were recruited from twelve general practices and randomised 1:1.

### Outcomes
The primary outcome was pain intensity measured on a 0 to 10 numeric rating scale at 8 weeks. Secondary outcomes were disability (Roland-Morris) and health-related quality of life.

### Statistical analysis
The primary analysis followed the intention-to-treat principle. A per-protocol analysis restricted to participants attending at least six of eight sessions was prespecified as a sensitivity analysis.

## Results
Two participants in the mindfulness arm and one in the usual care arm withdrew consent and were excluded from all analyses.
