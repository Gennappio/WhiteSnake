# P02. Supervised aquatic exercise for knee osteoarthritis: a randomised trial

## Abstract
Eighty-four adults with symptomatic knee osteoarthritis were randomised to twelve weeks of supervised aquatic exercise or an education booklet. The primary outcome was the 6-minute walk distance at 12 weeks.

## Methods
### Design and participants
Single-centre, two-arm, parallel-group randomised trial. Randomisation was 1:1, stratified by sex.

### Outcomes
The primary outcome was the 6-minute walk distance (metres) at 12 weeks. Secondary outcomes were WOMAC pain and stiffness subscales.

## Results
Four participants (one in the exercise arm, three in the booklet arm) withdrew before the 12-week assessment.

Table 1. Participant flow by arm

| Arm | Randomised | Analysed (ITT) |
|---|---|---|
| Aquatic exercise | 42 | 41 |
| Education booklet | 42 | 39 |

The mean between-group difference in 6-minute walk distance at 12 weeks was 31 metres (95% CI 12 to 50) favouring aquatic exercise.
