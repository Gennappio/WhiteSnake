# P01. Early mobilisation after hip fracture surgery: a randomised controlled trial

## Abstract
We randomised 120 older adults undergoing hip fracture surgery to early mobilisation within 24 hours or usual care. The primary outcome was the Timed Up and Go test at 30 days.

## Methods
### Participants and randomisation
Participants were randomised 1:1 to early mobilisation (EM) or usual care (UC) using a computer-generated sequence with concealed allocation.

### Outcomes
The prespecified primary outcome was the Timed Up and Go test (seconds) at 30 days after surgery. Secondary outcomes were length of stay and falls at 90 days.

## Results
Five participants were lost to follow-up before the 30-day assessment and are excluded from the analysed population.

Table 1. Participant flow by arm

The adjusted mean difference in Timed Up and Go at 30 days was -4.1 seconds (95% CI -7.0 to -1.2) favouring early mobilisation.
